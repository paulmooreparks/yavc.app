# Sight Reader sample scores

These are the sample scores Sight Reader offers, sorted by how hard they are to read: simple (one staff, a tune you may already know, in each clef), beginner (where the treble and bass clefs are read together, and longer single-line parts), intermediate and advanced. Add or remove files freely, then run `node tools/make-index.js` so `index.json` lists them; the folders are only a guide. Songs with a voice part were taken out of the samples on 2026-10-17 and kept in `backup/scores-with-voice-2026-10-17`, outside the site. The Lieder below are cut down to their piano part, so no voice staff is left in them.

## Typeset for this site

The single-line tunes in `simple/` with the `.musicxml` extension (Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star; Ode to Joy; Frère Jacques; Au clair de la lune; Mary Had a Little Lamb; Hot Cross Buns; London Bridge; Jingle Bells), and their versions in the bass, alto and tenor clefs, were typeset for parkscomputing.com in 2026. The tunes themselves are in the public domain.

## OpenScore

The other scores come from OpenScore's corpora (the Lieder and the String Quartets), which release their editions under Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0), so they may be used for any purpose. OpenScore asks for credit and a link, given here. The licence text is in `licenses/OpenScore-CC0-1.0.txt`. Each file was converted from MuseScore's format to compressed MusicXML with MuseScore's own converter by the MusicXML collection at https://codeberg.org/HerrBenedikt/MusicXML, and renamed here.
