# Lantern Line

Defend the Lantern outpost against descending rows of raiders. Fire through gaps in the four shelters, which crumble when hit. Each new wave starts lower, and the remaining raiders move faster as their numbers fall.

Use Left and Right, or A and D, to move. Space or X fires. Enter starts a game. P or Escape pauses and resumes it. Touch controls offer the same actions.

The game pauses when its window loses the keyboard. High scores and the sound setting remain available from the game menu. The old standalone address continues to open Lantern Line through the site's alias support.
