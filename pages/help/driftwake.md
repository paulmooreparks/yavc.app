# Driftwake

Steer through the debris field and break rocks into smaller pieces. Your ship keeps drifting after you stop thrusting. Rocks and ships wrap around the screen, and hostile saucers cross the field while firing at you.

Use Left and Right, or A and D, to rotate. Up or W applies thrust. Space or X fires. Z or Shift makes an emergency jump. Enter starts a game. P or Escape pauses and resumes it. Touch controls offer the same actions.

The game pauses when its window loses the keyboard. High scores and the sound setting remain available from the game menu. The old standalone address continues to open Driftwake through the site's alias support.
