# About the author

yavc.app is made by Paul Parks, a software developer in Singapore who has written C# and .NET for most of his working life and Go for the last few years, and who has rather more projects on the go than is sensible. His own site, with articles, tools and the games that first ran there, is at [parkscomputing.com](https://www.parkscomputing.com/).

Every control on this desktop comes from [PUDL](https://github.com/paulmooreparks/pudl), the Pleasantly Usable Design Language, which is his too. The applications are PUDL applet packages, most of them with repositories of their own under [github.com/paulmooreparks](https://github.com/paulmooreparks), and the engine behind this site is at [github.com/paulmooreparks/yavc.app](https://github.com/paulmooreparks/yavc.app).

If something here is broken, or you have an idea for it, write to [paul@parkscomputing.com](mailto:paul@parkscomputing.com).
