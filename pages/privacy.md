# Privacy policy

*Last updated 3 November 2026.*

yavc.app is a desktop of applications at yavc.app, and each application also runs on its own at its subdomain, such as sudoku.yavc.app. Paul Parks runs the site as an individual in Singapore, and he decides what it does with your data. You can reach him about anything on this page at [paul@parkscomputing.com](mailto:paul@parkscomputing.com).

The site keeps as little about you as it can. It has no advertising, it does not track you across other sites, it does not sell your data, and it shares your data with no one except the two services listed below: the network service it needs in order to work, and a visitor counter that keeps no personal data.

## The session cookie

The site sets one cookie, and it is the only one. The cookie is an anonymous session identifier on the yavc.app domain, so your browser sends it to the desktop and to every application's subdomain, and the same session follows you between them.

The cookie is set the first time you change something the site keeps for you. That means your appearance choices, which are the mode, the skin and the colour theme, and each application's state, such as a game in progress or an application's settings. Until you change one of those, no cookie is set.

Nothing in the cookie identifies you. It is a random value, and the site uses it for nothing but finding the state you asked it to keep. A session that has not been seen for {{retention}} is removed, together with everything kept under it. You can remove it yourself at any time with the Forget button in Settings, which deletes the session and its state at once.

## Files you make

Files you make in the editor, in the terminal or through an application's saves are kept in your own browser's local storage, and they are never sent to the site. Because local storage belongs to the address where a file was made, a file saved on the desktop is not seen at an application's subdomain, and the other way round. Clearing your browser's site data removes these files.

## What the server sees

The server sees each request your browser makes, as every web server does. It keeps ordinary access logs, which record the requesting address, the time and the page requested, for a short period and only for operating the site. Those logs are not joined to your session.

Every page is served from yavc.app, and the fonts come with the site. The one script loaded from elsewhere is Plausible's visitor counter, described below. The site loads no advertising and no other third-party scripts or fonts.

## Services the site uses

Cloudflare passes all traffic between your browser and the server, so it sees each request, including your IP address, as any network carrier does. The site uses Cloudflare under its standard data protection terms, which require it to protect your data to a standard comparable to Singapore's.

Plausible Analytics counts visits, so Paul can see which pages and applications people use. Each page loads its script from plausible.io, which records the page's address, the site that linked to it, the browser, operating system and kind of device, and the country the visit came from. It sets no cookie, stores nothing in your browser, does not keep your IP address, and does not follow you from one site to another, so the counts cannot be tied to you. Plausible keeps the counts on servers in the European Union. A content blocker that stops the script stops the counting, and the site works the same either way.

## Accounts

There are no accounts yet, and the site keeps no email address, name or password for anyone. When accounts arrive, this policy will change to say what an account keeps, and the date at the top will change with it.

## Your choices

You can change your appearance choices and each application's state yourself, in Settings and in the applications. The Forget button in Settings removes your session and everything kept under it. Files you have made are removed by clearing your browser's site data.

For anything else, or to ask what the site keeps about you, write to [paul@parkscomputing.com](mailto:paul@parkscomputing.com). You will get an answer within 30 days. If you think the site has mishandled your data, you can complain to Singapore's Personal Data Protection Commission, or to the data protection authority where you live.

## Security

All traffic is encrypted. The session cookie is marked so that scripts on the page cannot read it and so that it travels only over encrypted connections. If a breach puts your data at risk, the site will tell you and the Personal Data Protection Commission as the law requires.

## Children

The site is not meant for children, and it collects nothing from anyone that would identify them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. A change that affects how your data is used will be announced on the site before it takes effect.
